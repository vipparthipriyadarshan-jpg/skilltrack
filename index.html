The Core Problem

Vocational curricula (ITIs & Polytechnics) take 3–5 years to update through bureaucratic committees. Meanwhile, industry demand shifts in months (EV Battery Tech, Cloud DevOps, AI, Digital Weaving), producing severe unemployability and faculty mismatch.

Curriculum Lag Faculty Deficit Oversupplied Courses
💡

The SkillTrack Solution

A continuous, evidence-based data intelligence platform that reads live job postings via Adzuna API, maps demanded competencies against vocational course syllabi using RapidFuzz fuzzy token matching, and generates localized faculty upskilling roadmaps.

Continuous Evidence Dual-Tier NLP Engine Regional Intelligence
🏆

Why It Wins PS 26134

Unlike static mockups, this platform includes a live Human-in-the-Loop calibration loop where employers validate gap ratings with Agree/Disagree votes, actively tuning matching confidence thresholds.

Dual-Tier NLP Extractor
